Curve: The set has a clear chapter structure: each island teaches one mechanic (overlap/exact-fit, reach, colour, void, stamp, colour-cross, smudged, cracked, deep-chain, then pairings), and each island has a teach, practise, boss, breather, twist, finale rhythm. Mechanics are introduced alone first and combined later, which is the right approach. Problems:

1. Difficulty is front-loaded wrongly in places. Island 1 is a gentle ramp, but L6 (boss) and L12 (finale) have only 83-85% casual win rates.
2. Several 'teach' levels (L37, L38, L45, L49, L50, L73, L85) hand over their key fact in the intent text. The boards are fine, but many of them solve with few passes (2-4), so the new idea is not really tested. 'Teach' levels need a board where the new rule is the only way in.
3. The first level of the 'colour' chapter (L25) is the only one with a 97% casual win rate. Colour is the biggest jump in the game, and L26-L36 cover it with no real step up beyond size.
4. Difficulty spikes late. The boss and finale levels (L78, L84, L87-L96, L99, L108, L120) have casual win rates of 56-63%. For a calm, premium audience, a casual win rate below 65% on hand-picked levels is a risk. Mix in more 80%+ levels between them.
5. Pass counts barely rise with level count. Islands 3-5 sit at 3-7 passes, and the deep-chain island is the only one that really raises them. The mid-game is padded with look-alike 'one anchor row then fill' boards.
6. Many pictures are mirror-symmetric (L1, L5, L9, L17, L19, L31, L37-L38, L43, L67, L71, L91, L106) even though the intents promise 'nothing mirrors'. Symmetric boards solve half as much and feel repetitive.
7. Chapter finales do not always test mastery. L24, L48 and L72 mostly retest the island's single trick plus size. L48's intent also claims reach, which the board barely uses.
8. Intent text often mismatches the board. Examples are L5 (says the starfish has one step, but the board is a plain symmetric star), L8 (not a regatta), L26 and L75 (claims about shapes), and L63 and L64 (the intents do not fully match).
9. Later islands (smudged, cracked) are introduced on a clean, easy puzzle first, which is good. But they then reuse the same 'find the anchor row, read the hidden line' pattern from L73-L120 with little variation.

Overall: the structure and theming are strong, and there is no sign of broken boards. The weakness is that too many levels follow the same anchor-and-fill pattern, and the late-game hit rates are harsh.
