**Rendering (blank board and empty buttons at puzzle start).** I found two causes the earlier repairs missed:
- **Icons:** they were made at runtime as base64 PNGs, which Android decodes late, so buttons showed as empty circles. The previous warm-up step couldn't help, because Android caches decoded images by draw size and it drew them at 4 px. A new `scripts/bake-icons.ts` turns the icon shapes (now in `src/ui/glyphs.ts`) into bundled PNGs in `assets/icons/`, which `Icon` and the tutorial hand now use.
- **Board:** it is one Skia canvas that can appear a few frames late. I added a plain paper-and-grid layer under it, so the first frame always shows a real grid. The preview confirms this.
- **Win card:** on 360×800-class phones the Gallery/Home buttons ran off the screen. The card now scales down to fit.
- **Home screen:** a new player's Daily card no longer shows a "0" streak badge, which looked like a placeholder.
